## Introduction

FreshCart is a smart fresh product delivery platform that connects local sellers with customers. This training guide provides comprehensive instructions for using the application based on your role. The platform offers features such as product browsing, cart management, order tracking, and real-time notifications.

## User Roles and Access

The FreshCart application supports four distinct user roles, each with specific permissions and functionalities:

1. **Customer**: Browse products, add to cart, place orders, track deliveries
2. **Seller**: Manage products, process orders, update inventory
3. **Delivery**: Accept delivery requests, update delivery status, manage routes
4. **Admin**: System management, user verification, content moderation

## Getting Started

### Registration
1. Navigate to the FreshCart website
2. Click on "Register" in the top right corner
3. Select your user role (Customer, Seller, Delivery, or Admin)
4. Fill in the required information:
   - Name
   - Email address
   - Password
   - Confirm password
5. For sellers, additional information is required:
   - Store name
6. Click "Register" to create your account

### Login
1. Navigate to the FreshCart website
2. Click on "Login" in the top right corner
3. Enter your email and password
4. Click "Login"
5. You will be redirected to your role-specific dashboard

## Customer User Guide

### Browsing Products
1. After logging in, you'll be directed to the homepage
2. Browse products by category using the navigation menu
3. Use the search bar to find specific products
4. Filter products by price, rating, or availability

### Adding Products to Cart
1. Click on any product to view its details
2. Select the quantity you wish to purchase
3. Click "Add to Cart"
4. A confirmation message will appear
5. Click "View Cart" to proceed to checkout or continue shopping

### Managing Your Cart
1. Click on the cart icon in the top right corner
2. Review the items in your cart
3. Adjust quantities using the +/- buttons
4. Remove items by clicking the trash icon
5. Click "Checkout" when ready to place your order

### Placing Orders
1. From the cart page, click "Checkout"
2. Review your order summary
3. Select or add a delivery address
4. Choose a payment method
5. Confirm your order details
6. Click "Place Order"
7. You will receive an order confirmation

### Tracking Orders
1. Navigate to "My Orders" from your profile menu
2. View the status of all your orders
3. Click on any order to see detailed information
4. Track real-time delivery updates

## Seller User Guide

### Setting Up Your Store
1. After logging in as a seller, you'll be directed to your store dashboard
2. Complete your store profile:
   - Add store description
   - Upload store logo
   - Set business hours
3. Navigate to "Store Settings" to configure delivery options

### Adding Products
1. From your seller dashboard, click "Add Product"
2. Fill in product details:
   - Product name
   - Description
   - Price
   - Stock quantity
   - Category
   - Upload product images
3. Click "Add Product" to save

### Managing Products
1. Navigate to "My Products" in your dashboard
2. View all your listed products
3. Edit product details by clicking the "Edit" button
4. Update stock quantities as needed
5. Remove products by clicking the "Delete" button

### Processing Orders
1. Navigate to "Orders" in your dashboard
2. View all incoming orders
3. Click on an order to view details
4. Update order status:
   - Processing
   - Ready for Pickup
   - Out for Delivery
5. Communicate with customers using the messaging system

### Financial Management
1. Navigate to "Earnings" in your dashboard
2. View your sales reports
3. Track pending and completed payments
4. Request withdrawals when eligible

## Delivery User Guide

### Accepting Delivery Requests
1. After logging in as a delivery person, you'll see available delivery requests
2. Review delivery details including:
   - Pickup location
   - Delivery address
   - Order contents
   - Estimated earnings
3. Accept deliveries that fit your schedule and route

### Order Pickup
1. Navigate to the seller's location
2. Verify order contents with the seller
3. Mark the order as "Picked Up" in the app
4. Proceed to the customer's delivery address

### Delivery Process
1. Arrive at the customer's address
2. Contact the customer if needed
3. Deliver the order and collect payment (if applicable)
4. Have the customer confirm receipt
5. Mark the delivery as "Completed" in the app

### Earnings Tracking
1. Navigate to "Earnings" in your dashboard
2. View your delivery history
3. Track completed deliveries and earnings
4. Request payments according to the platform's payment schedule

## Troubleshooting

### Login Issues
- **Problem**: Unable to log in
- **Solution**: 
  1. Verify your email and password
  2. Reset your password using the "Forgot Password" link
  3. Check your internet connection
  4. Clear your browser cache and cookies

### Registration Issues
- **Problem**: Registration fails
- **Solution**:
  1. Ensure all required fields are filled
  2. Check that your email is not already registered
  3. Verify password meets requirements (at least 6 characters)
  4. Contact support if issues persist

### Payment Problems
- **Problem**: Payment processing fails
- **Solution**:
  1. Check your payment method details
  2. Ensure sufficient funds
  3. Try an alternative payment method
  4. Contact your bank if the issue persists

### App Performance Issues
- **Problem**: Slow loading or freezing
- **Solution**:
  1. Check your internet connection
  2. Close other applications
  3. Refresh the page
  4. Try a different browser

## FAQ

### How do I reset my password?
1. Click "Login" on the homepage
2. Click "Forgot Password"
3. Enter your email address
4. Check your email for a reset link
5. Follow the instructions to create a new password

### How long does delivery take?
Delivery times vary based on:
- Distance between seller and customer
- Current order volume
- Traffic conditions
- Typically, deliveries are completed within 30-60 minutes

### What payment methods are accepted?
FreshCart supports:
- Credit and debit cards
- Digital wallets
- Cash on delivery (where available)
- Platform-specific payment methods

### How do I contact customer support?
1. Navigate to your profile
2. Click "Help & Support"
3. Submit a support ticket
4. Alternatively, email support@freshcart.com

### Can I cancel an order?
Order cancellation depends on the status:
- Before confirmation: Automatic cancellation
- After confirmation: Contact the seller directly
- During preparation: Cancellation may be possible
- After dispatch: Cancellation not possible

### How do I become a seller?
1. Register as a seller during account creation
2. Complete the seller registration form
3. Upload required business documents
4. Wait for admin approval
5. Set up your store profile

### How are delivery persons paid?
Delivery persons are paid based on:
- Distance traveled
- Order value
- Delivery time
- Platform commission structure
Payments are processed weekly through the selected payment method.
